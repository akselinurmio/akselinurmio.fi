## How I work

- I take <b>ownership of my work</b> from day one — from planning all the
  way through the full lifecycle of the product or code.
- I aim to make everything I build <b>accessible</b> to everyone, in every
  situation. I create services people can rely on.
- I focus on making digital services <b>easier to use</b>. Tech can be
  complicated, but most of the time, it really doesn't have to be.
